## 4. CSS architecture

Cascade order is unchanged: tokens → base → motion → components → sections.

- **Tokens.** The Stage 3 diff was applied to `tokens.css` and is therefore
  site-wide — see § 11 D1 for why it was not scoped. `--scene-length` is the
  one motion token added.
- **Components** hold what is page-agnostic and owns an a11y contract or is
  used twice: the layer bodies, the rows, the tuner row, the note.
- **Motion** holds P6 whole: geometry, rail, mapping, timeline, backstops.
- **Sections** hold the scene layouts and the phone crops, all under one
  "PHASE 6" heading. Where a landing-page class collided with a name a
  sub-page still uses, the landing page took a modifier: `.argument--scene`
  (`/product/matching` keeps `.argument`). `.pool` and `.control` were
  home-only and were replaced.
- **Retired:** `.hero*`, `.interlude`, `.problem-statement`, `.gap__cards`,
  `.hero__composition`, `.bothsides`, `.philosophy`, `.hire__steps` — deleted
  with their markup. The tuner's and the pipeline's phone rules, `.adjacency`
  and `.cta--tight` stay because sub-pages compose them.
- No `!important` was added. Every landing-page selector is scoped to a scene
  class, a component class, or `html.js`. Specificity is at most four classes.

## 6. Animation architecture

- **P6** (`motion.css` § 4b): six absolutely positioned layers on a sticky
  stage 2.6 viewports tall; `--p` 0 → 1; boundaries 0.14 · 0.31 · 0.48 · 0.65 ·
  0.82; window 0.055; non-overlapping departure/arrival windows; the 87 → bars
  transition at 2 → 3 only; the persistent 87 in states 4–5; only `transform`
  and `opacity`.
- **Two clocks.** `@property --p` and a `view()` scroll timeline where
  supported (Chromium); `scene.js`'s passive listener elsewhere, using the
  same range: `top − header` to `top + height − viewport`. `scene.js` writes
  `data-state` and the rail in both cases.
- **The header variable (brief § 13).** `view(block 64px 0px)` is the one
  hard-coded number. `scene.js` reads `--header-h` from the computed root
  style, so the listener cannot drift. `tools/check.mjs` asserts the
  stylesheet's inset equals the token, and that every stage's
  `data-thresholds` equals the mapping's boundaries. A header change fails
  the check until `motion.css` follows. Verified: at eighteen sampled
  positions the native `--p` and the pin range agree within 0.012.
- **`will-change`** is on the six layers only while the stage is within a
  viewport of its pin (`is-near`); the audit asserts six promoted while
  pinned and none with the stage off screen.
- **Stack as base.** The pin is scoped to `html.js … :not(.sig--static)`;
  the media backstops (`prefers-reduced-motion`, `< 700px`, `< 620px` tall)
  force the stack before `scene.js` runs.
- **P5** in the hero is released on `is-open` from `main.js` (there is no
  reveal ancestor, by design), and `is-settled` releases its hint 600ms later.
- Nothing else new: no library, no timer, no autoplay, no loop, no
  scroll-hijack.

## 11. Known deviations

Every difference between the built prototype and production, with its reason.

**D1 — The token diff is site-wide, not page-scoped.**
Deviation: `proto-tokens.css` was applied to `tokens.css` for all 23 pages
(warm paper, the type ramp, the sans lede, the mono floor, no frame dots).
Reason: STAGE-3.md § 5 and § 13 say the diff "becomes the diff for
tokens.css"; the header and footer are shared, and a page on one paper under
a header on another is a seam. Brief § 5 permits global tokens "required by
the approved design".
Impact: every sub-page's H1 is up to 20px larger and its ground warmer. The
full audit passes on all of them; `/product/matching` and `/check/job-description`
were opened and read correctly at 1440 and 390. Not every sub-page was viewed
by eye.
Approved / unresolved: **unresolved** — a founder's glance at the sub-pages
is owed. The alternative (scoping the diff to `body.landing`) is a one-file
change if preferred.

**D2 — The tuner's fixture is isolated, not adopted.**
Deviation: production `RANKINGS` is untouched; the landing page reads
`rankings-proposed.js` via `data-rankings="proposed"`.
Reason: brief § 25–26 and STAGE-3.md § 12. The decision note stays on the page.
Impact: five sub-pages' tuners still never reorder for the advertised move
and still score gated candidates — the pre-existing finding, unchanged.
Approved / unresolved: **resolved 28 Sep 2026** — the source settled both
questions; the proposed model was adopted into `RANKINGS` and the isolated
file deleted (§ 12). The sub-pages' tuners now gate too.

**D3 — Two clocks use the pin range; the prototype's listener did not.**
Deviation: `scene.js` measures `track = height − viewport + header`; the
prototype measured `height − viewport`.
Reason: the pin ends when the stage's bottom reaches the viewport's bottom,
which is `top + height − viewport`; the native timeline's contain range ends
there too. The prototype's listener reached 1.0 sixty-four pixels early
where the timeline was unsupported. Measured: native and listener now agree
within 0.012 at eighteen positions.
Impact: none where the native timeline runs (Chromium); Firefox and Safari
now match Chromium exactly.
Approved / unresolved: implementation decision, made.

**D4 — Skill rows carry `--i`.**
Deviation: the built prototype's `layerSkills` set no `--i`, so its state 4
rows arrived together; production sets it, so they arrive in order.
Reason: STAGE-3.md § 3.2 and § 8 ("row i opacity clamp(a·2.4 − i·0.28)") and
the handoff ("`.srow` … with `--i`") all specify the order; the omission was
in the prototype's markup, not its design.
Impact: the state 4 entrance now staggers as designed.
Approved / unresolved: implementation decision in favour of the handoff text;
worth one glance.

## 12. Product decisions — settled 28 Sep 2026

Both of STAGE-3.md § 12's questions were settled by reading the product source
(`transpahire-backend`, `transpahire/frontend`), and the PRODUCT DECISION
REQUIRED note under the tuner was removed in the same change.

- **A. The critical gate — the gate is real; `RANKINGS` was wrong.**
  `MatchingService.isCriticalDisqualified` drops any candidate whose CRITICAL
  skill has `matchType === 'none'`, for cache hits and misses, before
  `RankerService.computeScore` runs ("a filter removes rows; it must never just
